Ideal for mythic, fantasy, adventure, and coming-of-age films (e.g., Star Wars, The Matrix).

## Ordinary World: Establishing the hero in their comfort zone.

## Call to Adventure: Disruptive challenge or quest.

## Refusal of the Call: Hesitation or fear.

## Meeting the Mentor: Guidance, training, or a gift.

## Crossing the First Threshold: Entering the special/unfamiliar world.

## Tests, Allies, Enemies: Learning the rules of the new world.

## Approach to the Inmost Cave: Preparing for the main ordeal.

## Ordeal: Facing the central crisis or threat.

## Reward (Seizing the Sword): Claiming the objective or power.

## The Road Back: Consequences and pushback on the return journey.

## Resurrection: The final test where the hero applies everything learned.

## Return with the Elixir: Returning home transformed, with something to benefit others.
